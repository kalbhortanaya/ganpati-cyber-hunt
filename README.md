# Cyber Hunt Quest

Create a mobile-first interactive cyber-safety treasure hunt web page called:

DIGITAL KAVACH

CYBER SAFETY HUNT

This is Mission 1 of a 6-part physical treasure hunt for a Ganeshotsav community event. Children and teenagers aged 8–18 will participate using smartphones.

IMPORTANT:

This is ONLY MISSION 1.

Do not create Missions 2–6.

This page must work completely independently because a separate QR code will open this page directly.

====================================================

DESIGN / VISUAL STYLE

====================================================

Create a fun, modern, colorful and premium-looking visual style inspired by a children's technology treasure hunt.

The design should NOT look like a boring school form or cybersecurity training website.

Use this exact visual direction:

BACKGROUND:

- Very light lavender / soft purple background

- Clean and spacious

- Subtle futuristic/cyber atmosphere

- Avoid dark full-screen cyberpunk styling

PRIMARY COLORS:

- Deep navy / very dark purple for text and borders

- Bright purple for the Digital Kavach branding and accents

- Bright yellow for the main action buttons

- Coral pink / bright pink for important information cards

- White/light lavender for content cards

HEADER:

Create a clean horizontal header at the top.

Left/center area:

A circular purple logo containing the letters:

DK

Next to it:

DIGITAL KAVACH

CYBER SAFETY HUNT

Use a bold, playful, modern font.

At the top/right of the header, display six small circular mission indicators:

1   2   3   4   5   6

Mission 1 should be visually highlighted as the active mission.

The other five should appear inactive/locked.

Under the header, add a thin dark navy horizontal line.

OVERALL STYLE:

- Thick dark navy outlines around important cards

- Rounded corners

- Playful geometric shapes

- Bold headings

- Large readable text

- Bright yellow CTA buttons with dark navy border

- Pink/coral information cards

- Purple accents

- Slight shadows

- Friendly animations

- Modern Gen Z / Gen Alpha aesthetic

- Fun but NOT childish

- Professional enough for a community event

The page must look excellent on a smartphone.

====================================================

TOP HERO SECTION

====================================================

At the top of the page, show:

MISSION 01

FAKE QR CODE TRAP

Use a large bold title.

Add a small subtitle:

THINK BEFORE YOU SCAN!

Add a short introductory line:

"Someone wants to trick you with a QR code.

Can you spot the trap?"

Use a visually interesting cyber/QR-code-inspired decorative element near the title, but DO NOT make the decorative QR code functional.

====================================================

CYBER PUZZLE CARD

====================================================

Create a large white/light card with a thick dark navy rounded border.

At the top of the card display:

🚨 CYBER PUZZLE

Then show the scenario clearly:

"You scan a QR code that promises a reward.

After scanning, the page asks:

'Enter your UPI PIN to receive your prize.'"

Then add a highlighted safety reminder:

"REMEMBER:

You NEVER enter a UPI PIN to RECEIVE money."

Make this reminder visually prominent.

====================================================

TASK CARD

====================================================

Below the scenario, create a bright pink/coral card.

Heading:

🔐 YOUR TASK

Then display:

UNSCRAMBLE THE LETTERS

Make the letters large and spaced out:

N  –  I  –  P  –  I  –  U

Below that ask:

"What does this spell?"

Provide a large mobile-friendly text input field.

Placeholder:

Type your answer here...

Below it, provide a large yellow button:

CHECK ANSWER

====================================================

ANSWER VALIDATION

====================================================

The correct answer is:

UPI PIN

The validation should:

- Ignore uppercase/lowercase differences

- Ignore extra spaces

- Accept "UPI PIN"

- Accept "UPIPIN"

- Accept "PIN UPI" if necessary

Do NOT reveal the next clue before the correct answer.

====================================================

WRONG ANSWER BEHAVIOR

====================================================

If the participant enters an incorrect answer:

Show an animated/friendly error state.

Display:

❌ NOT QUITE!

Then:

"Think about the secret number you should NEVER share just to receive money."

Then show:

TRY AGAIN

The participant should be able to enter another answer.

IMPORTANT:

Do not reveal the location clue when the answer is wrong.

====================================================

CORRECT ANSWER BEHAVIOR

====================================================

When the participant gives the correct answer:

Show a satisfying success animation.

Display:

🛡️ MISSION CLEARED!

Then:

"You spotted the QR scam!"

Add a small cyber safety tip card:

CYBER SAFETY TIP

"Never enter your UPI PIN just to receive money."

Then reveal the next clue.

====================================================

NEXT LOCATION CLUE

====================================================

The next clue MUST remain hidden until the correct answer is submitted.

After successful completion, display a visually exciting section:

🔓 NEXT LOCATION UNLOCKED

Then display this EXACT clue:

"I sparkle in the sunlight and dance without feet.

I have no water, but I am pool.

People gather near me where they get a cool breeze."

Below it display:

📍 FIND YOUR NEXT QR HERE

FOUNTAIN

Make the word:

FOUNTAIN

very large, bold and visually prominent.

This is extremely important because participants must physically travel to the Fountain to find the next QR code.

DO NOT automatically navigate to another page.

DO NOT provide a link to Mission 2.

The participant must physically find the next QR code.

====================================================

GAME FLOW

====================================================

The complete flow must be:

Participant scans QR CODE 1

        ↓

Mission 1 opens directly

        ↓

Reads cyber puzzle

        ↓

Unscrambles N-I-P-I-U

        ↓

Enters answer

        ↓

Wrong answer → retry

        ↓

Correct answer → Mission Cleared

        ↓

Next Location Clue appears

        ↓

Participant goes to FOUNTAIN

        ↓

Finds and scans QR CODE 2

This page must NOT require:

- Login

- Account creation

- Email

- Name

- Phone number

- Personal information

Keep it quick and easy to use during a live event.

====================================================

BOTTOM SECTION

====================================================

At the bottom of the page add a small footer:

DIGITAL KAVACH

CYBER SAFETY HUNT

THINK • VERIFY • STAY SAFE

And:

JAI GANESH 🙏

====================================================

IMPORTANT FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

====================================================

1. The page must be fully responsive.

2. It must look especially good on smartphones.

3. The answer input must be easy to tap.

4. The Check Answer button must work.

5. Wrong answers must allow retry.

6. Correct answer must unlock the clue.

7. The clue must remain hidden before the correct answer.

8. Do not automatically move the participant to another mission.

9. Do not collect personal information.

10. Do not add unrelated cybersecurity questions.

11. Keep the language simple enough for ages 8–18.

12. Make the experience feel like a real treasure hunt, not a Google Form.

====================================================

VERY IMPORTANT CONTENT RULE

====================================================

Do NOT change the following mission content:

MISSION:

THE FAKE QR CODE TRAP

ANSWER:

UPI PIN

NEXT LOCATION:

FOUNTAIN

NEXT LOCATION CLUE:

"I sparkle in the sunlight and dance without feet.

I have no water, but I am pool.

People gather near me where they get a cool breeze."

These are the source-of-truth details for Mission 1.

QR CODE

====================================================

Create the page so that it has a clean dedicated URL such as:

/mission-1

The QR code itself does NOT need to be displayed on this page.

The QR code will be physically printed and placed at the starting location.
